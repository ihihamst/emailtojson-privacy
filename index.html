EmailToJson

A personal Windows tool that turns your recent email into JSON for programs running on the same computer.

What it does

  • Reads mail from your own accounts (Gmail, Outlook, Hotmail, MSN, Yahoo) over IMAP, covering at most the last 7 days.
  • Returns it as JSON through a local API that only programs on the same computer can reach.
  • Withholds one-time-code emails, emails that contain malicious content, and unsafe attachments.

How it uses your Gmail account

  • You sign in with Google in your own browser. The app never sees your Google password.
  • Google asks you to allow full mail access (https://mail.google.com/), because that's the only permission Gmail's IMAP accepts. The app only ever reads mail. It never sends, deletes, moves or changes anything in your mailbox.
  • Everything happens on your computer. There is no EmailToJson server, and nothing is sent to the developer.

Who it's for

EmailToJson is a self-hosted tool for personal use. It isn't offered as an online service and has no user accounts.
