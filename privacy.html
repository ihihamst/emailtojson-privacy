EmailToJson

Privacy policy

Effective 10 November 2026

EmailToJson runs entirely on your own computer. Your email and Google account data stay there: nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Data the app accesses

  • Messages in the mail accounts you add (headers, text, and image or text attachments), from the last 7 days at most.
  • Your email address, which you type in when adding an account.
  • The sign-in tokens Google issues when you allow access.

How the data is used

Only to give the programs you run on the same computer a JSON copy of your recent mail. It isn't used for advertising, profiling, training AI models, or any other purpose.

Where the data goes

  • The app connects only to your mail provider's servers (for Gmail: imap.gmail.com and Google's sign-in servers).
  • Its API accepts connections only from programs on the same computer, never from the network.
  • There is no developer server. The app has no analytics, telemetry, cookies or ads.
  • No person reads your mail, and your data is never sold or given to third parties.

Storage and retention

  • Mail is never written to disk. It's held in memory, kept for at most 5 minutes, and then discarded.
  • Nothing older than 7 days is fetched.
  • Sign-in tokens and settings are stored encrypted in the app's own folder. They are tied to your computer and your Windows user, so copies of the files can't be read anywhere else.

Google user data

EmailToJson's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Removing an account in the app deletes its stored sign-in token.
  • You can revoke the app's access at any time in your Google Account under Third-party apps & services.
  • Deleting the app's folder removes all of its data.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Use the developer contact shown on Google's sign-in screen for EmailToJson.
